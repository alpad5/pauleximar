# CLAUDE.md

## Communication style
- Keep answers short and concise.
- Convey the general idea; skip verbose explanations unless asked.

## Session logging
- Maintain a running log in `SESSIONLOG.md`.
- Before wrapping up a session (or at a natural stopping point), append a dated
  entry with a short, descriptive summary of what was done.
- If anything worth remembering long-term came up, also update memory.
